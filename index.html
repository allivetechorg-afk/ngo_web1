Who We Are

Rooted in Bamenda, Growing for the Future

Bamenda Community Action Initiative (BCAI) is a non-profit organization founded to address socio-economic challenges, promote peace, and unlock technological and ecological potential across the North-West Region of Cameroon.

Our Mission

To empower youth and vulnerable communities in Bamenda through accessible digital skills, sustainable educational backing, environmental conservation, and peacebuilding dialogue.

Our Vision

A resilient, technologically proficient, and peaceful society in Cameroon where every young person has the tools and ecosystem to thrive and lead positive transformation.

Core Values

  • Community-Driven Ownership
  • Inclusion & Equity
  • Transparency & Integrity
  • Innovation for Impact
What We Do

Our Core Programs & Initiatives

Designed specifically to tackle grassroots challenges across Bamenda and surrounding divisions through targeted action pillars.

Technology

Digital Skills & Coding Bootcamps

Equipping Bamenda youth with foundational computer literacy, web development, and coding masterclasses to connect them with remote opportunities and tech-driven self-employment.

Over 1,200 Certified Join Next Cohort
Resilience

Community Resilience & Peacebuilding

Facilitating inter-generational dialogue forums, conflict resolution workshops, and mental health support circles to strengthen community cohesion across neighborhoods.

45+ Dialogue Circles Learn More
Environment

Environmental Sustainability & Tree Planting

Combating soil erosion and deforestation through community-led tree planting campaigns, climate awareness clubs in local schools, and sustainable waste management training.

10,000+ Saplings Planted Join Campaign
Education

Educational Support & School Supplies

Distributing school kits, textbooks, and learning materials to underprivileged children in Bamenda to ensure continued education despite local socio-economic disruptions.
